# Phase 23 — Eyes & Multimodal Perception
# Master Plan — EXACT

## 0. Existing foundation

The current perception path is primarily structural and explicitly does not provide Vision/OCR.
Phase 18 supplies deterministic accessibility/DOM control; Phase 23 adds bounded visual observation
for cases where pixels, layout, images, charts or deictic references are genuinely required.

## 1. Goal

Deliver consent-driven desktop eyes that combine accessibility structure, OCR and visual reasoning
into source-labelled observations without turning screenshots or model confidence into success proof.

## 2. Invariants

- Capture is user-visible, scoped and revocable; full-desktop continuous surveillance is not default.
- Password, payment, private/incognito and configured sensitive surfaces are blocked or masked.
- Local structural/accessibility evidence is preferred before pixels or remote providers.
- Every observation records source, target, time, bounds and confidence; fact and inference differ.
- Screenshots and visual judgements cannot directly produce VERIFIED; Phase 16 remains authoritative.
- Raw captures are bounded, redacted and non-durable by default; exports require explicit approval.
- Multimodal perception does not alter Phase 18 control safety or create a second action owner.
- Installer, packaging, product completion and public release are explicitly outside Phase 23.

## 3. Delta order

```text
23A Capture, Consent & Privacy Contract
→ 23B Accessibility Fusion & OCR
→ 23C UI, Image, Chart & Document Understanding
→ 23D Deictic Reference & Spatial Grounding
→ 23E Observation Replay & Evidence Separation
→ 23F Privacy/Multimodal Final Gate
```
