# Phase 19 — Connected Services & Communications
# Master Plan — EXACT

## 1. Goal

Connect communication, collaboration, media and life services behind one governed channel layer:
receive, normalize, summarize, draft and—with explicit authorization—send to a proven destination.

## 2. Authority boundaries

- Provider/platform data is external observation, not Persona/Memory truth.
- Contact identity is immutable and provider-qualified; display name alone is insufficient.
- Draft/recommend/send are separate capabilities.
- Incoming content cannot issue tool instructions or grant authority.
- Single Mouth owns user-facing speech; channels cannot independently impersonate Furina.
- Platform policy, rate limits and anti-abuse rules are mandatory.

## 3. Delta order

```text
19A Channel / Contact / Destination Contract
→ 19B Enterprise Providers
→ 19C Consumer Desktop Channels
→ 19D Media & Content Services
→ 19E Unified Inbox, Product UI & Final Gate
```

## 4. Product evidence

At least one real enterprise/provider integration and one controlled desktop-channel integration
must be proven. Unsupported or legally/technically unsafe platforms remain explicitly unavailable.
